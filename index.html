How it works

From live moment to scheduled Short

Surgeclip runs while the stream does. You come back to a pile of rated clips, not hours of footage.

  1. 01

    Watch

    Pick the streamers you clip. Surgeclip records their Twitch streams while they're live.

  2. 02

    Catch

    Chat, audio and the picture point to the moments that pop, as they happen.

  3. 03

    Rate

    AI scores every candidate, so the strongest clips are waiting at the top of your pile.

  4. 04

    Post

    Approve, pick the channel and a slot. YouTube uploads go out on schedule.

Your video stays yours

The heavy work runs on your PC

Recording, editing and rendering happen on your own Windows PC. The cloud only holds your account and the lists you want on the web.

On your PC

  • Recordings, clips, renders and edits
  • Channel sign-ins, in Windows Credential Manager
  • Your own AI keys, if you bring them

In your account

  • Profile, plan and credit balance
  • Creators, campaigns and schedule, when sync is on
  • Export or delete it all, any time
