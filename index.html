Surgeclip

Surgeclip is a small private tool. It uploads and schedules videos on its owner's own YouTube channels and other social accounts, from a desktop app and from AI assistants through a local connector.

It is not open to the public right now, and there is nothing to sign up for or buy.

How it works

  • The app runs on the owner's own computer. Videos, sign-ins and settings stay on that computer.
  • It uses the official YouTube Data API to upload videos, set titles and descriptions, and schedule when they go public.
  • It only acts on accounts the owner signed in to, and only when the owner asks it to.

Read the privacy policy and the terms of use. Questions: support@surgeclip.app.
