1. The short version

  • Surgeclip is a desktop app. Your video, recordings, clips, renders, transcripts, chat logs and editor projects stay on your PC. We never receive them, except the parts of a clip sent for AI work when you use credits (section 4).
  • Channel sign-ins (YouTube and others) and your own AI keys stay on your PC in Windows Credential Manager. They are never sent to us.
  • Our cloud holds your account, plan and credits, an AI usage log, and, when sync is on, lists such as channels, creators, campaigns and your post schedule, so you can see them on the web.
  • We don't sell your information, don't show ads, and don't use your data or Google user data to train AI models.
  • You can export everything we hold about you, or delete your account, from your account page.

2. What stays on your PC

The app keeps these only on your computer: recordings and downloaded videos, clips and renders, thumbnails, transcripts and captions, stream chat logs, editor projects, the app's settings and file locations, and the sign-in tokens for YouTube, Twitch and other platforms. The app contacts those platforms directly from your PC to watch streams and post videos. You control and can delete these files at any time.

Before anything is synced, the app strips sign-in tokens, keys and file paths, and our server refuses any record that still contains one.

4. AI requests

  • With included credits, the app sends each AI task to our server, which forwards it to our AI provider, Google's Gemini API, and sends the answer back. A request can include the instructions, text such as a transcript excerpt, and frames or a short video of the clip being rated. We do not store the request or its media. We keep the answer for a short time, at most [7 days], so a retried request isn't charged twice. We use Google's paid API service, under which Google does not use these requests to improve its products [confirm against Google's current Gemini API terms before launch].
  • With your own key, the app sends the request from your PC straight to the provider you chose, under your agreement with them. We don't receive those requests or their answers.
  • With an AI assistant connected through the app's MCP server, that assistant runs under your own account with its provider; we are not part of that exchange.

5. Google and YouTube data

Surgeclip lets you sign in with Google and connect YouTube channels using YouTube API Services.

  • Google sign-in gives us your email address, name and profile picture. We use them only to run your account.
  • YouTube access lets the app on your PC upload, schedule, read the status of and pull back videos on the channels you connect. The access token stays on your PC and is never sent to our servers.
  • From YouTube, our cloud stores only the channel's name and handle, whether it's connected, and the details of posts made with Surgeclip (title, scheduled time, status and public link), when sync is on.
  • Surgeclip's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to theGoogle API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google user data only to provide and improve the features you use. We don't sell it, use it for advertising, use it to train AI models, or transfer it except as needed to provide those features, for security, or to comply with the law. Our staff don't read it unless you ask us to for support, it's needed for security, or the law requires it.
  • YouTube's use of your data is covered by theGoogle Privacy Policy. Using Surgeclip's YouTube features means you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • You can remove Surgeclip's access at any time atmyaccount.google.com/permissions or by disconnecting the channel in the app. When access is removed, Surgeclip deletes the YouTube data it stored for that channel within 7 days. YouTube data we keep is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days.

6. How we use information

  • To run Surgeclip: sign-in, plans and credits, AI features, sync and the website account pages.
  • To keep it safe: preventing fraud and abuse of free credits, enforcing plan limits, investigating problems.
  • To support you and send account messages, such as sign-in links, receipts and low-credit warnings.
  • To send product news, only if you turned on product emails. You can turn them off any time.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting duties.

Where a law such as the GDPR applies, we rely on our contract with you (running Surgeclip), our legitimate interests (security and improving the service), your consent (product emails) and legal obligations (accounting records).

8. How long we keep it

  • Account, profile, synced lists and activity log: until you delete them or your account.
  • Synced items you delete: removed from your lists at once, and fully erased after 30 days, so every signed-in PC learns about the delete.
  • Stored AI answers: at most [7 days] (section 4).
  • Credit and usage records: kept for [N years] as accounting records. When you delete your account they keep only a random account ID, never your email or name.
  • Backups: encrypted daily backups are kept for 30 days, then deleted.

9. Export and delete

  • Export: download a copy of everything we hold about you as one file from your account page (profile, PCs, plan, credit history, usage for the last 365 days, synced lists and activity log).
  • Delete: delete your account from your account page by typing your email to confirm. Cancel any paid plan first. Deleting signs out every PC and erases your profile, picture, workspace, synced lists and activity log, and your sign-in, at once. We keep one record that an account was deleted, with counts only. Credit and usage records stay as described in section 8, without your email.
  • Files on your PC are yours to delete. After an account delete the app also removes the YouTube data it copied.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your information, to object to or limit some uses, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. California residents can ask what we collect and ask us to delete it, and we don't sell or share personal information for targeted advertising. Most of this you can do yourself from your account page; for anything else email[Contact email]. We'll answer within 30 days and won't treat you differently for asking.

11. Security

Every connection is encrypted. Each user can read only their own rows in our database, enforced by the database itself. Sign-in uses one-time codes, and the website keeps its session only until you close the tab. Payment card details never reach us. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we'll tell you as the law requires.

12. Cookies and the website

This website uses no advertising or analytics cookies and no trackers. The account pages keep your sign-in in your browser's session storage until you close the tab, plus a short-lived sign-in code for up to 15 minutes. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

13. Children

Surgeclip is not meant for anyone under 16. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

14. International transfers

We and our providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where the law requires, we use safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

15. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you by email or in the app before the change applies. The version and date at the top always show the current one.
