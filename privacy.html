Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Surgeclip is a private desktop tool that uploads and schedules videos on its owner's own channels. This policy explains what data it uses and where that data lives.

The short version

  • Surgeclip runs on the owner's computer. It has no cloud server and no user database.
  • Sign-in tokens, videos and settings are stored only on that computer.
  • We don't sell data, show ads, share data with third parties, or use any data to train AI models.

YouTube API Services

Surgeclip uses YouTube API Services. By using Surgeclip you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's use of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

When you connect a YouTube channel, Surgeclip asks Google for permission to upload videos and to manage the channel's videos (the youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl scopes). It uses that access only to:

  • read the channel's name, handle and ID, so you can pick which channel to post to;
  • upload the videos you choose, with the title, description, tags, privacy and schedule you set;
  • list the channel's recent and scheduled videos, and change their title, description, privacy or schedule when you ask.

Surgeclip does not read comments, analytics, subscribers or any other YouTube data, and does not access any Google data beyond the above.

Instagram, Facebook and TikTok

If you connect an Instagram, Facebook or TikTok account, Surgeclip uses that platform's official API only to read the account's name and ID, publish the posts you choose, and show their status. That data is handled the same way as YouTube data: it is kept only on your computer.

What is stored and where

  • Sign-in tokens for each connected account, in a private folder on your computer.
  • A short upload history (file name, channel, video ID, time), so the same video isn't posted twice by mistake.
  • Your videos stay wherever you keep them. Surgeclip reads a file only when you upload it.

None of this is sent to Surgeclip or anyone else. Data goes straight from your computer to Google, YouTube or the platform you post to.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Remove an account in the Surgeclip app (or with its "disconnect" command). That deletes the account's saved sign-in from your computer.
  • Revoke Surgeclip's access to your Google account at any time at Google Account permissions. For Instagram or Facebook, use the "Apps and websites" settings of your Meta account. For TikTok, use "Manage app permissions" in the TikTok app.
  • Uninstalling Surgeclip and deleting its folder removes everything it stored. Videos you already posted stay on the platform until you delete them there.

Surgeclip keeps no copy of your data anywhere else, so there is nothing else to delete. If you'd like help, email support@surgeclip.app.

Security

Sign-ins use each platform's official OAuth flow. Surgeclip never sees your passwords. Saved tokens can be read only by your own user account on your computer.

Children

Surgeclip is not meant for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
