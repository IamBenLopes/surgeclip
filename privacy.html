Privacy policy

Signalcut · last updated November 19, 2026

Signalcut is a desktop app that runs only on its owner's own computer. It has no servers, no accounts and no other users. It does not collect, sell or share any personal data, it shows no ads, and it does not use any platform's user data to train AI models.

What Google data it uses

When the owner signs in with Google, Signalcut receives access to the owner's YouTube account with these permissions: upload videos (youtube.upload) and manage the owner's YouTube videos (youtube.force-ssl). It uses them only to read which channel the account owns, upload clips the owner approved, check their status, and delete an upload when the owner asks.

Signalcut's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

What Meta (Instagram and Facebook) data it uses

When the owner signs in with Facebook, Signalcut receives the permissions the owner allows: list the owner's Pages (pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, business_management), post to them (pages_manage_posts), and read and post to the Instagram professional accounts linked to them (instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish). It uses them only to show the owner which Page or Instagram account a channel posts to, publish clips the owner approved, and check whether each post went out.

What TikTok data it uses

When the owner signs in with TikTok, Signalcut receives the permissions the owner allows (user.info.basic, video.publish, video.upload). It uses them only to show the owner's TikTok name and posting options, upload clips the owner approved, and check whether each post went out.

Where it is kept

All sign-in tokens are saved only on the owner's computer, in the app's local data folder. They are never sent anywhere except to the platform they belong to, when calling that platform's API.

How to remove access and delete data

The owner can sign out of any platform inside Signalcut (Settings, Channels), which deletes that platform's saved tokens from the computer. Access can also be removed at any time on the platform itself: Google account permissions, Facebook Settings → Business integrations / Apps and websites, and TikTok Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. Signalcut keeps no copy of any user data anywhere else, so nothing else needs to be deleted. Questions: open an issue at github.com/IamBenLopes/signalcut.
