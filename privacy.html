Privacy policy

Signalcut · last updated November 19, 2026

Signalcut is a desktop app that runs only on its owner's own computer. It has no servers, no accounts and no other users.

What Google data it uses

When the owner signs in with Google, Signalcut receives access to the owner's YouTube account with these permissions: upload videos (youtube.upload) and manage the owner's YouTube videos (youtube.force-ssl). It uses them only to read which channel the account owns, upload clips the owner approved, check their status, and delete an upload when the owner asks.

Where it is kept

The sign-in tokens are saved only on the owner's computer, in the app's local data folder. They are never sent anywhere except to Google when calling the YouTube API. Signalcut does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and it does not use Google user data for advertising or to train AI models.

How to remove access

The owner can sign out inside Signalcut, which deletes the saved tokens, or remove Signalcut's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Signalcut's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
