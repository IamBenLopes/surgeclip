Live capture

Records while they're live, so you never chase a VOD

Add the Twitch streamers you clip. While they're live, Surgeclip keeps a rolling recording on your PC and saves the stretch around every big moment, setup included.

  • Keeps the lead-up and the reaction, not just the second chat exploded
  • Watch several channels at once, each with its own sensitivity: Low, Normal or High
  • Reads public streams and chat, so no streamer needs to sign anything in
  • Clears old recordings on its own, so your drive doesn't fill up

Moment detection

Chat points to the moment, Surgeclip checks it

Surgeclip compares chat speed with each channel's normal pace and counts how many different people join in. Then it reads what kind of reaction it is and looks at the picture for what's on screen.

  • Counts unique chatters, so one person spamming can't set it off
  • Sorts reactions into laughs, hype, shock and "clip it" calls
  • A viewer's own Twitch clip of the same moment counts as a strong vote
  • Finds the facecam and the game HUD, like the minimap and GTA wanted stars

AI clip rating

The best clips are waiting at the top

Every moment gets an AI score and a one-line reason. A clip only reaches Review when several signals agree; the near misses wait in a Maybe pile for you to judge.

  • Four checks per clip: score, payoff, crowd reaction and a hook a stranger gets
  • Top picks first, with filters for streamer, check and score
  • Open any Maybe clip in the editor, then keep it or drop it
  • You approve every clip. Nothing posts on its own

Vertical editor

Framed and captioned before you open it

Clips come out 9:16 with the streamer's face found and framed above the gameplay, and word-timed captions on. Change anything you like in the editor, and the render runs on your own PC.

  • Facecam found for you, with stacked and split layouts
  • Word-by-word captions in the style you pick, with slurs bleeped and starred
  • Trim with one-tap nudges and cut dead air without losing the punchline
  • Sound levelled for Shorts, and renders that never wait in an online queue
  • Montages: join 2 to 12 approved clips into one video

Scheduling

Fill each channel's best slots at a steady pace

Give each channel its own posting times for every day of the week, in the order they should fill. Approved clips drop into the next best free slot, inside that channel's daily cap and spacing.

  • Per-channel times, daily caps and minimum gaps between posts
  • YouTube uploads go up private and scheduled, so you can pull one back before its slot
  • AI drafts the title and description; a title you type always wins
  • TikTok: ready-to-post files you upload yourself

Campaigns

Keep every campaign and submission straight

Paste a campaign link and Surgeclip reads the pay, budget, platforms and rules into a draft. Link clips to campaigns as you approve them, and see what still needs submitting before the window closes.

  • Campaign hashtags and mentions go into the post text for you
  • A submit list with the post link to copy and the time left
  • You submit on the campaign site; Surgeclip keeps the record
  • Check campaigns and notes from your phone in your account Soon

Creators and long video

Know who you clip, and mine the long streams

Add a creator from their profile links and see when they're live and what they last posted. Tag them, group them, and attach a campaign to a whole group. For long streams, scan a past broadcast and let AI find the storylines.

  • Profiles from pasted Twitch and YouTube links; other socials saved as links
  • Tags from the games and categories they stream, plus your own
  • Content groups: a group's campaign shows on every member
  • Scan a past broadcast for story arcs, or ask for one in your own words
  • Linked clips: build one Short from several moments of the same stream
