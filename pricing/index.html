Credits

How credits work

One balance pays for every AI action the app runs for you. Your account page shows each call and the credits it used.

One credit is half a cent

1,000 credits = $5

Rating one clip uses about 0.1 to 1 credit, depending on its length and the model. So 6,000 credits on Clipper Pro rate at least 6,000 clips.

Monthly credits first, top-ups after

MonthlyTop-up
  • Monthly credits refill at the start of each billing month and reset, so they don't roll over.
  • Top-up credits never expire and are only used once the monthly ones run out.

Your own key

Add a key from Google Gemini, OpenRouter or another supported provider on any plan. Calls on your key are billed by your provider and never touch your credits.

When you run Surgeclip from your own AI assistant over MCP, the assistant's own replies run on its plan, not on your credits.

Questions

Pricing questions

Are these prices final?

Not yet. These are the planned Early Access prices and they may change before Early Access opens.

Once you pay for a plan, we tell you at least 14 days before any price change reaches it, and you can cancel before it applies.

What happens when I run out of credits?

AI work such as rating pauses. Recording, the editor, rendering and your schedule keep working.

It starts again when your monthly credits come back, when you upgrade or, on a paid plan, buy a top-up, or as soon as you add your own AI key.

Do unused credits roll over?

Monthly credits reset at the start of each billing month, so unused ones don't carry over.

Top-up credits are different: they never expire. Each month your monthly credits are used first, then your top-ups.

How does my own AI key work?

You paste an API key from an AI provider you already pay, such as Google Gemini or OpenRouter. The app sends its AI calls with that key and your provider bills you directly.

Your own key works on every plan, and calls on it never use credits.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. When you upgrade in the middle of a month you get the extra monthly credits straight away, not a second full allowance.

When you downgrade, you keep what you have this month and the smaller allowance starts next billing month.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time from your account. Your plan runs to the end of the month you paid for, then moves to Rookie, the free plan. Top-up credits you already bought stay in your balance.

If Surgeclip doesn't work for you, you can ask for a refund of your first payment within 14 days. The terms have the details.

Which platforms can Surgeclip post to?

Surgeclip records from Twitch and posts to YouTube, uploading Shorts as private and scheduled so you can pull one back before its slot.

For TikTok it exports the finished vertical file for you to upload. Instagram and Facebook Reels posting is coming soon.

Why do renders cost nothing?

Recording, detection, captions, editing and rendering all run on your own Windows PC, so they cost us nothing and we don't meter them. Credits only pay for AI calls made through our servers.
