TwitchLive

Watch the streams you clip, live

Add the streamers you clip. Surgeclip records them on your PC while they're live and follows their chat, so a moment is caught as it happens.

  • Records each live stream on your own PC, ready to cut the second a moment lands.
  • Reads the live chat: a burst of hype, laughter or emotes flags the moment.
  • Shows who is live now and finds live streamers in the games you clip.
  • Reads past broadcasts for storylines and longer clip ideas.

What you need

  • No Twitch sign-in. Surgeclip only reads public streams and chat, and never posts to Twitch.
  • For now, a free Twitch developer key for live alerts and finding streamers. The setup guide walks you through it.

Good to know

  • Your PC needs to be on to record. Each stream you watch uses bandwidth and disk while it runs.
  • Clip only streamers whose content you have the right to use, such as a campaign that pays for clips.

YouTubeLive

Shorts that go up on schedule

Approve a clip, confirm the channel and Surgeclip uploads it to YouTube as a private video that goes public in your next open slot.

  • Uploads straight to YouTube: private and scheduled, with your title, description and the paid-promotion label when a clip is sponsored.
  • Per-channel posting slots, daily caps and spacing, so a channel never gets a flood.
  • Pull a clip back any time before its slot and it never goes public.
  • Your channel's numbers from YouTube Analytics, and its comments with replies that only go out when you click Reply.

What you need

  • A YouTube channel, and a Google sign-in for each channel you post to.
  • Your sign-in stays in Windows Credential Manager on your PC. It never reaches our cloud.

Good to know

  • Google gives every app a daily upload allowance. If it runs out, clips wait for the next day (midnight Pacific) instead of failing.
  • Once a Short is public, only YouTube Studio can take it down.

TikTok, Instagram and Facebook

More places to post, one step at a time

TikTok works today through export. Direct posting to Instagram and Facebook Reels is built and waiting on Meta's app review. We'll mark each one Live only when you can use it.

TikTok

Export

Every approved clip is saved to a folder with a ready-to-paste caption and hashtags. Upload it in TikTok, or line it up with TikTok's own scheduler.

  • The video and a text file, named by date
  • Caption and hashtags ready to paste
  • Direct posting: soon, after TikTok's approval

You need a TikTok account. Nothing to connect for export.

AI providers and MCP

Pick who powers the AI

AI rates every clip, writes post text and reads long broadcasts. Use the credits in your plan, bring your own key, or drive the whole app from your own AI assistant.

Included credits

Nothing to set up. Every plan comes with monthly AI credits that run on Google Gemini through Surgeclip, and the app shows what each job used.

  • Rating a clip uses about 0.1 to 1 credit
  • We don't keep your prompts or video
See plans and credits

Your own key

Already pay for AI? Paste your key and Surgeclip calls the provider straight from your PC. You pay the provider, not us.

  • Google Gemini
  • OpenRouter
  • Qwen (Alibaba Cloud)
  • GLM (Zhipu)
  • Choose a provider per job: rating, post text, long video
  • Keys stay in Windows Credential Manager, never in our cloud

AI apps on your PC

Signed in to an AI command-line tool on this PC? Surgeclip can send its jobs there and use the plan you already have.

  • Gemini CLI
  • Claude Code
  • Runs on your PC under your own sign-in
