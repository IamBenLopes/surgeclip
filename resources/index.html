Getting started

From install to your first clip

Four steps, and most of it is waiting for the models to download. After that Surgeclip watches the streams for you.

  1. 1

    Install Surgeclip

    Run the Windows installer. The first start takes a few minutes while Surgeclip sets up its speech and detection models.

  2. 2

    Sign in

    Surgeclip opens your browser so you can sign in with Google or an email link. The app never sees your Google password.

  3. 3

    Connect YouTube

    Pick the channel you post to and allow uploads. The sign-in stays on your PC, in Windows Credential Manager.

  4. 4

    Add streamers

    Paste the Twitch links of the creators you clip. Surgeclip watches for their next stream and starts recording when they go live.

Help and support

Questions, answered

Does Surgeclip post anything without me?

No. Every clip waits in Review until you approve it, and you choose the channel it goes to.

Which platforms does it work with?

Surgeclip clips Twitch streams. It uploads Shorts straight to YouTube on your schedule. For TikTok it exports a finished file you upload yourself. Posting to Instagram and Facebook Reels is being built.

See all integrations

Does my video go to the cloud?

No. Recording, editing and rendering run on your own PC, and your clips stay in your folders. Your account only holds your profile, plan and credits, plus your creators, campaigns and schedule if you turn sync on.

Read the privacy policy

Do I need my own AI key?

No. Plans with included credits pay for the AI that rates clips and writes post text. If you already pay an AI provider, you can paste your own key instead.

Use your own AI key

What is a credit?

Credits pay for AI work done for you. One credit is about half a US cent of AI, and rating one clip uses about 0.1 to 1 credit, depending on its length. Plan prices are still being set for Early Access.

Compare plans

Does it run on Mac or Linux?

Not yet. Early Access is for Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. Surgeclip records and renders on your PC, so a capable machine helps.

What your PC needs

Which streams can I clip?

Clip streams you have permission for: campaigns you've joined, streamers who welcome clippers, or your own channel. Every clip carries a credit to the streamer that can't be hidden in the editor. If a streamer asks you to stop, block them in Settings and Surgeclip stops clipping them.

When does Early Access open?

Soon. Downloads and sign-ups open on this site when it does; until then the buttons say Coming soon.

How do I export or delete my data?

From your account settings you can download everything stored in your account, or delete the account and its cloud data. Clips on your PC are yours to keep or delete.

Something isn't working. What should I send?

Open Settings, General, Diagnostics and choose Export diagnostics. It saves a file with logs, setup checks and versions, and leaves out keys, tokens, email addresses and personal folder names. Attach it to your email.
