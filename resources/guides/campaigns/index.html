Campaigns guide

Track campaigns and submissions

Paste a campaign link, let Surgeclip read the pay and rules, link clips to it and tick off every submission.

4 min readSurgeclip for Windows, Early Access

On this page

  1. What a campaign is
  2. Add a campaign
  3. Campaign rules
  4. Link clips to a campaign
  5. Submit and tick it off
  6. Check it from anywhere

What a campaign is in Surgeclip

Clipping campaigns pay you to post clips of a creator or a brand, usually by views. In Surgeclip a campaign is one row in your list: its title, pay, budget, status, the platforms it pays for and its rules, tied to the creator or group of creators it's about.

Surgeclip keeps the list for you and reminds you what still needs submitting. It never joins a campaign or submits a clip for you: "Joined" and "Submitted" are ticks you set yourself.

Add a campaign

  1. Open Creators and switch to Campaigns.
  2. Paste the campaign's link. Pages from Content Rewards and Reach are read best, but any campaign page works.
  3. Surgeclip reads the page and fills in a draft: title, pay, budget, status, platforms and the rules it found. Nothing is saved yet.
  4. Pick the creator or group the campaign is for, check the details, and save.

Tip

If no rules were found, the draft says so. Open the campaign page and read the rules there before you post: they are what the campaign pays on.

Campaign rules

Each campaign can carry the rules it asks for. The ones Surgeclip reads and shows as pills on the campaign:

  • Hashtags and mentionUsed when Surgeclip writes the post text for that creator's clips, so you don't forget them.
  • No logos, no AI, no linksReminders of what the campaign won't accept in the video or the caption.
  • Footage and minimum viewsWhich footage counts, and the views a post needs before it pays.
  • Submit withinHow soon after posting you have to submit the link.

Status follows the campaign: active, upcoming, paused or ended. Ended campaigns stay in the list so your history is complete.

Link clips to a campaign

When you schedule a clip, the Schedule box asks which campaign it's for. Tick one campaign, several (one clip can count for more than one), or none. The link is what lets Surgeclip remind you to submit it later.

Submit and tick it off

Once a linked clip is live, it shows on its Posted row with every campaign still waiting on it.

  1. Press the orange submit button. Surgeclip copies the post link and opens the campaign page.
  2. Paste the link on the campaign's site and submit it there.
  3. Back in Surgeclip, tick Submitted, or Skip if you decided not to submit it. Ticked something by mistake? Undo shows for a few seconds.

Good to know

Nothing is ever sent to a campaign site from Surgeclip. The submission is yours, made on the campaign's own page.

Check it from anywhere

Turn on sync and your campaigns and submissions also appear in your account on this website, so you can check pay, rules and notes from your phone.
