Editor guide

Fine-tune a clip in the editor

Scenes, sources and transform, laid out like a streaming studio. Frame the webcam, style captions and render on your PC.

6 min readSurgeclip for Windows, Early Access

On this page

  1. The layout
  2. Scenes
  3. Sources
  4. Transform, hook and captions
  5. Timing and keyframes
  6. Checks and Render
  7. Keyboard shortcuts

The layout

Surgeclip makes every clip vertical (9:16) with the webcam framed and captions on, so many clips need no editing at all. When one does, the editor is laid out like a streaming studio, so it feels familiar if you've used one.

  • StreamThe original stream, where you draw the areas you want (webcam, game, map).
  • OutputThe Short itself, 1080 × 1920, playing live as you change things.
  • TransformThe exact numbers for whatever you've selected.
  • Scenes, Sources, ControlsAlong the bottom, with the timeline strip underneath.

Tip

Nothing you do in the editor posts anything. Render is the only button that makes a video, and the clip still waits for your approval in Review.

Scenes

A scene is a saved arrangement: where every source sits, plus the look (colours, fonts, caption style). Click a scene and the clip switches to it at once; Ctrl + Z switches back.

  • Five built-in scenes (Stack, Stack split, Portrait, Center and Fit) are the layouts Surgeclip uses on its own. Duplicate one to make your own version.
  • Press + to save what you see as a new scene.
  • Use for makes new clips pick a scene by themselves: for one channel, one game, or every clip. A channel scene beats a game scene, which beats "every clip".
  • Try on other clips previews a scene on up to six recent clips side by side, without saving or rendering anything.

Sources

Sources are everything on the Short, and the one at the top of the list is drawn on top. Add a webcam, game or map area, a custom area, the whole stream, a blur area, text, an image or a colour block.

  • The eye hides a source and the lock stops it moving by accident.
  • Drag sources up or down to change the order. Hook, captions and credit keep a fixed place.
  • Right-click a source for quick fixes: fit or fill the frame, centre it, reset the crop, or add filters like blur, darken, rounded corners and a border.
  • The credit to the streamer can't be hidden.

Area not found?

If a streamer moves or hides their webcam, the area shows "Not found on this clip". Redraw it on the Stream view, or choose what should happen when it's missing: hide it, use the normal layout, or show the whole stream.

Transform, hook and captions

Select a source and Transform shows its position and size in pixels, its crop from each edge, and how it fills its box: fill it, show all of it, follow the face, or exactly the area you drew. One source carries the clip's sound.

The hook is the short line at the top that tells viewers what's about to happen (60 characters at most). Captions have their own size, style and colours.

Timing and keyframes

Trim the clip in the timeline strip under the editor. Each source can also have its own Shows from and Until times; a negative number counts back from the end, so −2 means two seconds before it ends.

Keyframes animate a source's position, size, opacity and zoom. Turn on Auto keyframe and every move you make writes a keyframe at the playhead; turn it off and changes apply to the whole clip.

Checks and Render

With nothing selected, Transform lists the clip's checks. Red checks stop Render until you fix them; amber ones are warnings. When it's clean, press Render. The video renders on your own PC, not in a queue on someone else's server.

Tip

Playback can look a little softer while it plays, to stay smooth. A paused frame is exact: what you see paused is what Render makes.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • SpacePlay and pause
  • Ctrl + Z / YUndo and redo
  • Arrow keysNudge 1 px, or 10 px with Shift
  • Ctrl + DDuplicate the selected source
  • Ctrl + ↑ / ↓Move a source up or down
  • EscDeselect

Surgeclip is in Early Access, so button names and screens can still change. If something here doesn't match what you see, tell us and we'll fix the guide.
