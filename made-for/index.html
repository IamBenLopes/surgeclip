Whoever you are

The same engine under every workflow

All three ways of working run on the same app, so everyone gets the parts that save the most time.

  • Records while they're live

    Pick the streamers. Surgeclip records their Twitch streams on your PC as they happen.

  • Rates every moment

    Chat, audio and the picture flag the moments; AI scores each one and says why.

  • Nothing posts without you

    Every clip waits for your approve, and you confirm the channel each time.

  • Your video stays on your PC

    Recording, editing and rendering run on your own Windows PC, not in someone's queue.
