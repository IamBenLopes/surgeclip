The job

Running clips as a service

An agency clips for a roster of creators and runs their Shorts channels, often alongside channels of its own. The work is the same as a single clipper's, multiplied by every channel.

What gets in the way

What breaks when you add channels

  • Every channel has its own rhythm

    Different creators, different best times, different limits. One shared calendar can't hold all of that.

  • Posts end up in the wrong place

    With many channels open at once, a clip meant for one creator lands on another's channel.

  • The roster keeps growing

    Who you clip, which group they're in, what each one asked for: it lives in someone's head or a spreadsheet.

  • Clients ask how it's going

    Answering means opening every channel's dashboard and comments, one by one.
