The job

One streamer, every day, in their style

Editing for a streamer on retainer is about keeping up: every stream covered, every clip on brand, and their channel never going quiet.

01

Every stream

Your streamer goes live on their schedule, not yours. Some streams run six hours, some start at midnight.

02

Their style

Clips have to look like their channel: the webcam framed right, their caption look, their logo, clean audio.

03

A steady feed

Their Shorts channel needs posts every day, spaced out, at the times their viewers are on.

What gets in the way

Why keeping up is hard

  • Streams don't wait for you

    The best moment happens at 3 AM, and by morning you are scrubbing a six-hour VOD to find it.

  • Stories get lost

    A bit that builds across a whole stream never fits one clip, and is easy to miss when you only skim.

  • The same edits, every clip

    Framing the webcam, fixing captions, bleeping slurs and cutting dead air, by hand, again and again.

On brand before you touch it
