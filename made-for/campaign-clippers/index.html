The job

Lots of creators, lots of campaigns, one of you

Campaign clipping pays by the view, so volume matters. A normal day has three parts, and each one takes longer than it should.

  1. 01

    Find the moments

    Watch hours of streams from the creators running campaigns and spot the parts worth clipping.

  2. 02

    Cut and package

    Turn each moment into a vertical Short with the hashtags, mentions and labels the campaign asks for.

  3. 03

    Post and submit

    Post on the right channels, then submit every post link before the campaign's window closes.

What gets in the way

Where the hours go

  • Hours of footage

    The good moments hide in long streams, and finding them by hand eats the day before you have cut anything.

  • Every campaign has rules

    Required hashtags, mentions, no other logos, a deadline to submit. Miss one and the clip doesn't count.

  • Submit windows close

    Posts have to be submitted within hours. With many clips across many campaigns, some slip through.

  • Clips lose their campaign

    Once a clip is posted it is hard to tell which campaign it was for and whether it went in.
