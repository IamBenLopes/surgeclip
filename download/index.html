Download Surgeclip

Install in four steps

  1. Run the installer and follow its steps. The first start can take a few minutes while Surgeclip sets up its speech and detection models.
  2. Sign in. Surgeclip opens your browser so you can sign in with Google or an email link. The app never sees your Google password.
  3. Connect your YouTube channel. Google asks you to allow uploads for the channel you pick. The sign-in stays on your PC.
  4. Add streamers. Pick the creators you clip, and Surgeclip starts watching for their next stream.

"Windows protected your PC"?

Early Access builds may not be code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen can warn about an unknown publisher. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Only do this for an installer you downloaded from this page.

Uninstalling

Remove Surgeclip from Settings, Apps. Your clips and recordings stay in your folders unless you delete them. To delete your account and its cloud data, use your account page.

What your PC needs

Surgeclip records, transcribes and renders on your own computer, so it likes a capable PC. These are Early Access guidelines and may change as we test more machines.

Minimum

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • 4-core CPU
  • 16 GB of memory
  • 15 GB free disk space, plus room for recordings and clips
  • A stable internet connection
  • One stream at a time; slower renders

Recommended

  • 8 or more CPU cores
  • 32 GB of memory
  • NVIDIA graphics card with 6 GB or more
  • An SSD for recordings
  • Suits clipping several streams at once

You'll also want

A YouTube channel

To post Shorts straight from Surgeclip. Other platforms get an exported file to upload yourself.

The right to post

Clip streams you have permission for, such as campaigns you've joined or your own channel.

Optional: your own AI key

Use included credits, or paste a key from an AI provider you already pay.
